# SlopShield Privacy Policy

SlopShield has no server and collects nothing.

**What the extension does with data:**

- Video transcripts and titles from your YouTube homepage are fetched by your browser directly from YouTube's player API, and sent to the AI provider you configured (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint you choose) to be scored. This happens with your own API key, from your own browser.
- Your API key and settings are stored locally in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) and are only ever sent to the provider you picked.
- Scores are cached locally in your browser.

**What is never collected:** the developer runs no servers, receives no data, and has no analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind. Your watch history, transcripts, key, and scores never reach anyone except the AI provider you explicitly configured.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/yak0v/slopshield
